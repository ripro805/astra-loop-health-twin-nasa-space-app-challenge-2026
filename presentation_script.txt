## Part 2: 5-Minute Comprehensive Judge Walk-Through

### Scene 1: Problem Definition & Operational Context (0:00 – 0:45)
- **Visual:** Landing Page (`/`) with the 5 NASA HRP Hazards mapped across deep space transit, Moon base, and Mars surface.
- **Narrator:**
  "Long-duration exploration missions fundamentally alter human physiology. Under microgravity, fluids shift toward the head, elevating intracranial pressure and causing SANS vision deterioration. Cosmic radiation and solar particle events damage bone marrow and immune cells. Prolonged isolation in hostile closed environments degrades sleep, mood, and cognitive latency.
  NASA's Human Research Program explicitly states: astronauts must be equipped to assess their own health. But astronauts are engineers and pilots, not necessarily flight surgeons. They need an intelligent, visually compelling, explainable system that translates raw sensor streams into actionable clinical decisions without fabricating data."

### Scene 2: The Interactive Clickable Human Body (0:45 – 1:30)
- **Visual:** Navigate to `/body`. Show hover effects, color-coded regions, and the Hazard Lens toggle.
- **Narrator:**
  "We address the challenge's request for a visual, interactive human body figure. Here in `/body`, our vector anatomical twin models 9 organ systems. 
  Notice the color codes: Green indicates nominal stability; Amber reflects monitoring; Red flags clinical attention. 
  When we toggle the **Hazard Lens**, the body dynamically re-colors to highlight which organs are under siege from specific hazards: Gravity unloading highlights cardiovascular baroreceptors and cancellous bone; Hostile Environment highlights respiratory ventilation and brain CO2; Radiation highlights immune lymphocytes and the lens.
  Clicking any organ system—like the Cardiovascular system—opens a detailed clinical dossier: resting heart rate, arterial blood pressure, biological mechanism of action, recommended countermeasures, and NASA-STD-3001 evidence citations."

### Scene 3: Mission Control Hero Dashboard & Dual-Layer Safety (1:30 – 2:30)
- **Visual:** Navigate to `/today`. Demonstrate persistent Escalation Banner and 7 domain cards.
- **Narrator:**
  "On the `/today` dashboard, we monitor 7 physiological domains: Cardio, Sleep, Mind, Muscle-Bone, Immune, Metabolic, and Environment.
  Here lies our key medical innovation: **Dual-Layer Safety Resolution**. 
  Most predictive AI uses purely personal baselines. But if an astronaut’s baseline is already abnormal—for example, chronic hypercapnia or creeping hypertension—a baseline-only model would falsely show 'Green'.
  ASTRA-LOOP pairs 14-day flight baseline EWMA Z-scores with **absolute clinical safety floors and ceilings** from NASA-STD-3001. If systolic blood pressure exceeds 140 mmHg or cabin CO2 crosses 3,000 ppm, absolute clinical safety immediately overrides personal baselines, triggering our prominent persistent Escalation Banner."

### Scene 4: Daily Routine Checklist & Ultrasound Scanner (2:30 – 3:15)
- **Visual:** Navigate to `/routine`. Complete a blood pressure task via quick entry, then open Camera/Ultrasound modal.
- **Narrator:**
  "NASA asked: How do we prompt astronauts to assess their health as part of daily routine?
  In `/routine`, tasks are structured into Morning, Afternoon, Evening, and Bedtime buckets. 
  Crew members enter readings with automated clinical safety checks. 
  Furthermore, we support camera and ultrasound image capture directly in the browser—allowing astronauts to record optic nerve sheath diameter (ONSD) scans or facial fatigue metrics, which are buffered in offline Delay-Tolerant Networking storage and transmitted to ground when link budget permits."

### Scene 5: Clinical Decision Trees & Learn Loop Verification (3:15 – 4:15)
- **Visual:** Navigate to `/guidance`, step through a Sleep Loss / Hypercapnia decision tree. Then show `/progress` gamification.
- **Narrator:**
  "When an anomaly is flagged, crew members don't have to guess what to do. The **Treatment Wizard** (`/guidance`) provides interactive decision trees for acute in-flight scenarios. Astronauts answer branching diagnostic questions and receive clear, tiered action protocols with exact countermeasure prescriptions and ground flight surgeon transmission notes.
  In `/progress`, we incorporate gamification with adherence streaks, level progression, and mission badges like 'Bone Guardian' and 'Steady Heart'—with safety caps ensuring astronauts are never rewarded for physical over-exertion.
  And in `/report`, a single click generates a consolidated flight surgeon medical dossier formatted for Earth medical operations."

### Scene 6: Conclusion (4:15 – 5:00)
- **Visual:** Demo modal switching from Spacecraft (0g) to Mars Base (0.38g).
- **Narrator:**
  "With multi-environment support across transit spacecraft, lunar stations, and Martian bases, ASTRA-LOOP provides a medically honest, fully explainable, offline-first health twin for humanity’s next giant leap. Thank you."
