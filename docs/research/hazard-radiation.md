# Research Note: Space Radiation (Hazard 1)
## NASA Human Research Program (HRP) Analysis

### Mechanism & Pathophysiology
Space radiation consists of three distinct ionizing radiation components:
1. **Galactic Cosmic Rays (GCR):** High-energy protons and heavy atomic nuclei (HZE ions) originating outside the solar system. Chronic, highly penetrating, difficult to shield completely.
2. **Solar Particle Events (SPE):** Intermittent, acute fluxes of low-to-medium energy protons following coronal mass ejections (CMEs) or solar flares. Can deliver life-threatening absorbed doses within hours to unshielded crew.
3. **Trapped Radiation Belts (Van Allen Belts):** Relevant during Earth departure and return trajectories.

### Physiological & Clinical Consequences
- **Acute Effects:** Acute Radiation Sickness (ARS), nausea, acute lymphocyte and granulocyte suppression, gastrointestinal distress.
- **Sub-acute & Delayed Effects (72-hour window):** Increased systemic oxidative stress, endothelial activation, sleep architecture fragmentation, elevated subjective fatigue, and sub-acute neuroinflammation impairing cognitive performance.
- **Long-term Risks:** Excess lifetime risk of fatal cancer, accelerated cardiovascular endothelial fibrosis, cataracts, central nervous system late effects.

### Onboard Spaceflight Measurement
- Active silicon dosimeter badges (continuous dose rate $\mu\text{Sv/h}$ to $\text{mSv/h}$).
- Passive thermoluminescent dosimeters (TLD) and optically stimulated luminescent dosimeters (OSLD).
- Real-time tissue equivalent proportional counters (TEPC).

### Escalation & Countermeasures
- **Nominal Deep Space Background:** $\approx 0.04 - 0.06\text{ mSv/h}$ ($\approx 1 - 1.5\text{ mSv/day}$).
- **Warning Threshold:** $\ge 0.20\text{ mSv/h}$ (triggering 72-hour multi-sensor watch window).
- **Critical SPE Threshold:** $\ge 1.0\text{ mSv/h}$ (triggering immediate storm shelter protocol).
- **Countermeasures:** Move to radiation-shielded habitat storm shelter (surrounded by water reservoirs and polyethylene shielding); reschedule extravehicular activities (EVA); monitor peripheral blood counts and clinical symptoms; administer targeted antioxidant and supportive hydration regimens.
