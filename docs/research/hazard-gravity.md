# Research Note: Gravity Fields (Hazard 4)
## NASA Human Research Program (HRP) Analysis

### Mechanism & Field Transitions
Exploration missions encounter multiple distinct gravitational regimes:
1. **Microgravity ($0\text{g}$):** Transit phases in deep space (e.g. Earth to Mars, 6–9 months).
2. **Lunar Surface ($0.166\text{g} \approx 1/6\text{g}$):** Partial gravity on Artemis basecamp.
3. **Martian Surface ($0.38\text{g} \approx 3/8\text{g}$):** Partial gravity on Mars base.
4. **Hyper-gravity ($3\text{g}$ to $5\text{g}$):** Launch, atmospheric entry, descent, and landing (EDL).

### Physiological Adaptation & Pathophysiology
- **Cephalad Fluid Shift:** In $0\text{g}$, hydrostatic pressure gradients disappear. $\approx 1.5 - 2.0\text{ liters}$ of venous blood and interstitial fluid shift from the lower limbs towards the thorax and head. Leads to "puffy face", nasal congestion, transient stroke volume elevation, central baroreceptor resetting, and spaceflight-associated neuro-ocular syndrome (SANS: optic disc edema, posterior globe flattening).
- **Musculoskeletal Unloading & Atrophy:** Without weight-bearing gravity:
  - Bone mineral density (BMD) decreases $\approx 1.0 - 1.5\%$ per month in cancellous bone (femoral neck, lumbar spine, calcaneus) due to uncoupled osteoclast resorption vs. osteoblast formation. Excess calcium enters circulation, increasing nephrolithiasis (kidney stone) risk.
  - Postural muscle atrophy (soleus, gastrocnemius, quadriceps, erector spinae) drops postural volume and peak aerobic capacity ($\text{VO}_2\text{ max}$).
- **Orthostatic Intolerance:** Upon re-entering a gravity field (landing on Mars or returning to Earth), venous pooling causes acute hypotension, presyncope, and syncopal collapse unless countermeasured.

### Onboard Spaceflight Measurement
- Daily exercise telemetry from ARED (Advanced Resistive Exercise Device), T2 treadmill, and CEVIS (Cycle Ergometer with Vibration Isolation and Stabilization System).
- Body mass measurement device (Space Linear Acceleration Mass Measurement Device - SLAMMD).
- Resting blood pressure and pulse pressure changes.
- Ocular ultrasound and retinal fundus imaging.

### Escalation & Countermeasures
- **Microgravity ($0\text{g}$):** High daily target: $150\text{ minutes/day}$ exercise ($60\text{ min}$ heavy resistive loading up to $600\text{ lbs}$ equivalent $+ 60-90\text{ min}$ aerobic interval training).
- **Moon ($0.16\text{g}$):** Partial loading offset: $120\text{ min/day}$ exercise requirement.
- **Mars ($0.38\text{g}$):** Partial loading offset: $90-100\text{ min/day}$ exercise requirement.
- **Pre-entry Fluid Loading:** Ingestion of isotonic electrolyte solutions ($1\text{ liter}$ water with $8\text{ g}$ sodium chloride) $2\text{ hours}$ before gravitational transition to restore intravascular blood volume.
