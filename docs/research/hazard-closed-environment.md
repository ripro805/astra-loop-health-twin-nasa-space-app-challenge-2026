# Research Note: Hostile Closed Environments (Hazard 5)
## NASA Human Research Program (HRP) Analysis

### Mechanism & Environmental Physics
Spacecraft cabins are sealed, recirculated artificial ecosystems. Without natural planetary atmospheric convection, ventilation depends on forced fans. Gases, moisture, heat, particulate dust, microbial flora, and acoustic vibration build up unless continuously scrubbed by Environmental Control and Life Support Systems (ECLSS).

### Physiological & Clinical Vulnerabilities
- **Carbon Dioxide ($\text{CO}_2$) Accumulation:** On Earth, outdoor air $\text{CO}_2 \approx 420\text{ ppm}$ ($0.04\%$). On ISS/spacecraft, human exhalation raises cabin $\text{CO}_2$ to $2000 - 4000\text{ ppm}$ ($1.5 - 3.0\text{ mmHg}$). In unventilated pockets, localized $\text{CO}_2$ bubbles form around sleeping astronauts. Clinical signs: frontal headache, cognitive slowing, visual disturbance, lethargy, increased intracranial pressure.
- **Thermal and Humidity Stress:** Cabin temperature ($18-27^\circ\text{C}$) and relative humidity ($30-70\%$). Low humidity ($< 25\%$) dries mucosal membranes and eyes, increasing microbial vulnerability. High humidity ($> 70\%$) promotes fungal growth on module bulkheads.
- **Continuous Acoustic Noise:** Life support pumps, fans, avionic cooling loops, and exercise hardware generate sustained ambient background noise ($55 - 70\text{ dBA}$), causing hearing threshold shifts, speech interference, and chronic sleep disturbance.
- **Microbial & Lunar/Martian Dust Toxicity:** Lunar and Martian regolith particles are abrasive, jagged, sub-micron silicates that cause acute pulmonary irritation, ocular abrasion, and chemical toxicity if tracked into habitats.

### Key Atmospheric Standards (NASA-STD-3001 & SMAC)
- **$\text{CO}_2$ Nominal:** $< 2000\text{ ppm}$ ($< 1.5\text{ mmHg}$).
- **$\text{CO}_2$ Warning:** $\ge 3000\text{ ppm}$ ($2.3\text{ mmHg}$) — triggers ventilation check and pacing advisory.
- **$\text{CO}_2$ Critical:** $\ge 5000\text{ ppm}$ ($3.8\text{ mmHg}$) — triggers emergency scrubber canister change and crew symptom check.
- **Acoustic Sleep Environment:** Maximum continuous sound level $< 45\text{ dBA}$ in crew quarters (NC-40).
- **Cabin Humidity:** Nominal $35 - 60\%$.
- **Cabin Temperature:** Nominal $20 - 24^\circ\text{C}$.
